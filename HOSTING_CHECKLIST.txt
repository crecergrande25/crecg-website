CRECG V6 — PRE-HOSTING CHECKLIST

1. Review the website locally and approve text/images.
2. Confirm final domain name.
3. From the website root run:
   python deployment/configure_domain.py https://YOUR-DOMAIN.IN
   This adds canonical URLs, og:url, Organization website/logo URLs, sitemap.xml and sitemap entry in robots.txt.
4. Upload the contents of this folder to the selected host.
5. Enable HTTPS and redirect HTTP to HTTPS.
6. Verify all phone, WhatsApp, email, GSTIN and Udyam details.
7. Test desktop and mobile navigation.
8. Test WhatsApp and email enquiry buttons on contact.html.
9. Test all PDF downloads.
10. Confirm 404.html is used by the hosting provider.
11. After launch: verify domain in Google Search Console, submit sitemap.xml, inspect homepage URL and request indexing.
12. If adding a third-party form backend later, update the Privacy Notice to name that service and how enquiry data is processed.
13. Replace representative/AI-generated imagery with approved real CRECG project/facility photographs whenever available.
