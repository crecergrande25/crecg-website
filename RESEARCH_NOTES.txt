  https://www.precitec.com/service/precitec-service-offer/spare-parts/
- TRUMPF: press-brake die bending uses an upper tool and V-shaped lower tool; standard and special bending tools are common tooling categories.
  https://www.trumpf.com/en_IN/products/machines-systems/bending-machines/
  https://www.trumpf.com/en_INT/products/services/services-machines-systems-and-lasers/tools/bending-tools/

  https://www.se.com/in/en/product-subcategory/3910-controllers-plc-and-pac-for-industrial-machines/
  https://www.se.com/us/en/product-subcategory/3930-input-output-i-o-modules-for-plc-and-pac-controllers/
- Siemens: SIMATIC IOT2000 is an industrial IoT gateway for connecting machines, IT systems and cloud platforms.
  https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/simatic-iot-gateways/iot2000/

  https://www.teyuchiller.com/fiber-laser-chillers_c2.html
