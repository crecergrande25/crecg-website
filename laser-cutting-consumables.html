Compatibility first

Identify the exact requirement before ordering.

Protective windows and other optics are not generic glass parts. Wavelength/coating, laser power and exact head position can matter, while nozzle styles and sizes vary by cutting-head design.

Please share

  • Machine make/model
  • Cutting-head make/model
  • Laser source type and power
  • Existing nozzle/optic part number
  • Nozzle thread and tip size if known
  • Lens diameter/thickness and position
  • Clear photos of old part and head
Common items

Typical products within this category.

The list is intended as a sourcing guide. Availability and suitability remain model-specific.

Item GroupWhat we can discuss / source
Cutting NozzlesSingle-layer, double-layer and model-specific/custom nozzle styles; nozzle tip size, thread and geometry confirmed against cutting-head requirements.
Protective / Cover GlassesProcess-side and other protective windows used to protect higher-value optics from spatter and contamination; dimensions and optical coating must match the head/application.
Ceramic Parts / RingsCeramic nozzle-holder and capacitive-sensing related parts used on applicable laser cutting heads.
Protective Glass Cartridges / HoldersDrawer-type or exchange cartridges/holders for compatible cutting heads.
Focusing / Collimating OpticsSelected compatible lens or lens-assembly requirements subject to wavelength, power, focal specification and head model.
Seals & Small Wear PartsO-rings, seals, retainers and related small items where specified for the particular cutting head.
Gas / Head Support SparesSelected pressure, gas-line, sensor and cutting-head support parts where identification information is available.
