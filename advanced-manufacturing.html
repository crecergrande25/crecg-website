Division overview

From engineering requirement to manufactured component.

CRECG coordinates appropriate manufacturing routes based on geometry, material, quantity, tolerance, finishing and delivery requirements. The division is especially useful where a customer needs more than one manufacturing process or needs a part developed from limited information.
