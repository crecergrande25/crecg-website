Frequently asked questions

Before you send the requirement

Do you need the physical sample?

A physical sample is preferred for detailed measurement. Photographs and known dimensions can support initial feasibility review.

Can you manufacture the replacement too?

Where suitable, the design can transition into machining, fabrication, 3D printing or other manufacturing support.

Can you guarantee original OEM geometry?

The recreated design is based on available evidence, measurements and functional requirements; unknown internal or proprietary OEM details cannot be assumed.

Have a reverse engineering of industrial & machine components requirement?

Send the available drawing, sample, files, photographs, machine details or problem statement.
