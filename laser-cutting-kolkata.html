What file is best for laser cutting?

DXF/DWG or a clear manufacturing drawing is preferred; other files can be reviewed for conversion.

Can cutting be combined with bending or welding?

Yes. Where required, cutting can be coordinated with bending, welding, machining, marking and finishing.

Do you stock every sheet grade?

Material availability depends on the grade, thickness, finish and quantity; this is confirmed during quotation.
