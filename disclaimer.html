Some website images are illustrative or AI-generated capability visuals. They are used to communicate process/product categories and do not necessarily depict CRECG-owned machines, a specific customer facility, or a completed customer project.

Product compatibility

Product photographs and category descriptions do not establish compatibility. Laser optics, nozzles, ceramic parts, pumps, sensors and other spares must be verified against the relevant machine/head/chiller model, dimensions, operating parameters and available OEM/technical information before supply.

OEM and brand relationship

Unless explicitly stated in a written quotation or agreement, CRECG should not be understood as an authorised dealer, authorised service centre or OEM representative of any third-party machine, laser, cutting-head, chiller or component brand.

Certification and registration services

CRECG may provide consulting, documentation, readiness and coordination support. Final certification, registration, licence, barcode allocation, government approval or accreditation decision remains with the authorised issuing organisation or independent certification body.

Inspection services

CRECG may provide inspection and QA support within an agreed scope. It is not represented on this website as an ISO/IEC 17020 accredited inspection body unless such accreditation is specifically obtained and stated.

Technical decisions

Final engineering, safety, suitability and operating decisions should be based on the specific application, applicable standards, OEM requirements and agreed project scope.
