Tender & GeM Support

Tender discovery, eligibility review, document preparation and bid-support services for manufacturers and suppliers.

Discuss RequirementView Profiles
Division overview

Turn public procurement opportunities into a structured workflow.

Many MSMEs lose opportunities because relevant bids are missed or documents are not prepared in time. CRECG can support the administrative and technical-documentation side while the client remains responsible for commercial decisions, declarations and final bid authorisation.
