Division overview

Systemise. Comply. Certify. Grow.

This division helps manufacturing and engineering businesses build practical systems and prepare documentation for customer, certification and market requirements. CRECG provides support and coordination; the authorised organisation remains responsible for issuing any certificate, registration, licence or identifier.

Udyam registration itself is completed through the official Government of India portal. GS1 identifiers are issued by GS1 India; IEC by DGFT; NSIC SPRS by NSIC subject to eligibility; BIS licences/certificates by BIS; ISO certificates by an independent certification body.

Core scope

What this division can support.

Final scope is confirmed against the actual requirement, technical information and available resources.

Management Systems

  • ISO 9001 QMS implementation
  • ISO 14001 / ISO 45001 / ISO 50001 readiness support subject to scope
  • SOPs, forms, registers and process documentation
  • Internal-audit and management-review support
  • NCR / CAPA and process-improvement systems

Product & Certification Coordination

  • Testing/documentation coordination where applicable
  • Vendor-registration documentation
  • Audit-finding closure support
