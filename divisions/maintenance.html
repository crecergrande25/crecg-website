Industrial Machine Maintenance

Breakdown maintenance, preventive maintenance and troubleshooting support for CNC, VMC, laser and other industrial machinery.

Discuss RequirementView Profiles
Division overview

Machine uptime needs practical troubleshooting.

CRECG can coordinate engineering and technician support according to machine type, fault information, location and scope. The model is suitable for breakdowns, planned preventive work and machine-related spare identification.

CRECG uses a flexible technical network. Engineer/technician deployment depends on the project and is not represented as a permanent on-site OEM service organisation.
