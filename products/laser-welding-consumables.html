Laser Welding Consumables

Protective cover glasses, welding nozzles, selected optical wear parts and wire-feed related consumables for industrial laser welding equipment.

Request QuoteView Profiles
Representative product-category visual. Final supplied item depends on confirmed specification.
Compatibility first

Identify the exact requirement before ordering.

Modern laser welding heads commonly use replaceable cover glasses to protect internal focus/collimation optics, and may use coaxial or side-blow nozzle arrangements. Wire feeders are application- and head-dependent.

  • Welding-head model
  • Laser source type and power
  • Existing cover-glass/nozzle dimensions
  • Wire feeder make/model if applicable
  • Filler wire grade/diameter if required
  • Photos and part numbers
