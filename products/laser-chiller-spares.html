Compatibility first

Identify the exact requirement before ordering.

Laser chiller water circuits commonly include a pump, flow switch/sensor, temperature probe, valves and filters. Correct coolant/water specification should follow the chiller and laser OEM requirements rather than a generic website recommendation.

Please share

  • Chiller make/model and full nameplate photo
  • Laser source power / machine model
  • Existing pump/sensor/filter part number
  • Voltage and frequency
  • Pump power, flow and head if available
  • Connector dimensions and photos
  • Alarm code / failure symptoms if relevant
