Frequently asked questions

Before you send the requirement

Can every component have a different QR code?

Yes. Variable-data marking can be coordinated when individual QR files or source data are supplied in a controlled sequence.

Can stainless steel be marked?

Yes, subject to material, finish, required contrast and the selected marking process.

Can QR readability be checked?

Basic post-marking scan/readability verification can be included when defined in the agreed scope.
