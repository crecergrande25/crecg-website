An Indian engineering and industrial services venture focused on practical, technically sound and commercially viable support for manufacturing and engineering businesses.

Our direction

Built as an extension of the customer’s engineering capability.

CRECG combines engineering capability, industrial experience and a flexible technical network to support customers across design, manufacturing, industrial components, maintenance, quality systems and business support.

Our objective is to understand the actual project requirement first and then connect the right capability — rather than force every enquiry into a single service model.

CRECG works through a combination of internal coordination and appropriate technical/manufacturing resources depending on the scope. Specialist execution may be performed through project-based technical partners where required.
