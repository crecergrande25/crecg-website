Compatibility before supply

Similar-looking parts are not always interchangeable.

Laser-machine optics and wear parts can vary by cutting/welding head, laser wavelength and power, dimensions, coating, mounting arrangement and machine design. Chiller parts likewise vary by pump duty, flow, pressure, voltage, connector and control design.

For the fastest quotation, share:

  • Machine make and model
  • Laser source power and type
  • Cutting / welding / marking head model
  • Chiller make/model and nameplate photo
  • Existing part number, dimensions and photographs
  • Quantity required

Important optics note

Do not select a laser lens or protective window only by diameter. Coating wavelength, laser power, optical position, thickness, head model and mounting arrangement can matter. CRECG will request the available technical information before proposing a compatible item.
