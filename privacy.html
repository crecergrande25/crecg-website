Privacy Notice

How information submitted through the website and Admin Center is handled.

Website enquiries

When the secure website backend is connected, information submitted through the enquiry form may be stored so Crecer Grande can review, respond to and track the enquiry. This may include your name, company, phone number, email address, requirement type, machine/model details, part number, quantity and the message you submit.

WhatsApp and email

If you choose the WhatsApp or email buttons, the website prepares your enquiry for the external service you choose to open. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Administration and security logs

The private Admin Center uses authenticated administrator accounts. Administrative content changes are logged with the administrator identity, action, affected area and timestamp for accountability and recovery purposes.

Website media and documents

Images or PDFs deliberately published through the website manager are intended for public website access. Confidential customer drawings, quotations or internal commercial documents should not be uploaded to the public website-media area.

Analytics and cookies

No advertising cookies are intentionally included in this package. The Admin Center stores an authentication session in the administrator's browser so secure login can function. If analytics, advertising or additional third-party services are added later, this notice should be reviewed and updated.
